# What’s new

## Windows 0.5.9 · Android 0.5.9 · 20 November 2026

- **Android:** a new **Developer mode** tile: one tap turns on Developer options and Wireless debugging, another tap turns them off so banking apps work; hold it to open Developer options. It also works on the Xiaomi Pad 6 now. Activating from your computer shows the spark animation on the phone. Switching between Bluetooth, the speaker and wired headphones restarts the sound cleanly, and Sukut recovers by itself if the sound keeps cutting out.
- **Windows:** Install Sukut opens Sukut's Google Play page on your phone and guides you (phones without Google Play get the included version). While activating, the phone shows the spark animation. Includes Android 0.5.9.
- Linux stays 0.5.7.

<div lang="ar" dir="rtl">

- **أندرويد:** زر جديد «وضع المطوّر»: لمسة تشغّل خيارات المطوّر والتصحيح اللاسلكي، ولمسة أخرى توقفها لتعمل تطبيقات البنوك، والضغط المطوّل يفتح خيارات المطوّر. يعمل الآن على Xiaomi Pad 6 أيضًا. التفعيل من الحاسوب يعرض حركة الشرارة على الهاتف. التبديل بين البلوتوث ومكبّر الصوت والسماعات السلكية يعيد تشغيل الصوت بسلاسة، ويتعافى سُكوت بنفسه إذا ظل الصوت يتقطّع.
- **ويندوز:** «تثبيت سُكوت» يفتح صفحة سُكوت في جوجل بلاي على هاتفك ويرشدك (الهواتف التي لا تحتوي على جوجل بلاي تحصل على النسخة المرفقة). أثناء التفعيل يعرض الهاتف حركة الشرارة. يتضمن أندرويد 0.5.9.
- لينكس يبقى 0.5.7.

</div>

## Windows 0.5.8 · Android 0.5.8 · 19 November 2026

- **Google Play:** Sukut for Android is now on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.altarafiyah.sukut).
- **Android:** one tap activates Sukut, with a spark animation while it turns on debugging and starts by itself. Without Wi-Fi, Sukut says so (no internet is needed; a hotspot is enough) and turns itself back on when Wi-Fi connects. Home says "Sukut is on" with what each mode keeps. Screen recordings no longer repeat the sound on Android 14 and older. A new step shows how to get Sukut for Windows or Ubuntu, and the icon picker is tidier on tablets.
- **Windows:** choose your app icon (five designs, nine colours) with a larger taskbar icon. The menu opens beside the page and stays open. Activate your phone over USB step by step, with a card for your phone at the top of the Phone page. The tray notice appears only once, and Standby is gone. Includes Android 0.5.8.
- Linux stays 0.5.7.

<div lang="ar" dir="rtl">

- **جوجل بلاي:** سُكوت لأندرويد متوفر الآن على [جوجل بلاي](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.altarafiyah.sukut).
- **أندرويد:** تفعيل سُكوت بلمسة واحدة مع حركة شرارة بينما يشغّل تصحيح الأخطاء ويبدأ بنفسه. عند غياب Wi-Fi يخبرك سُكوت بذلك (لا حاجة للإنترنت، ونقطة اتصال تكفي) ويعود للعمل بنفسه عند الاتصال. الشاشة الرئيسية تقول «سُكوت يعمل» مع ما يبقيه كل وضع. لم يعد الصوت يتكرر في تسجيل الشاشة على أندرويد 14 وما قبله. خطوة جديدة لتثبيت سُكوت على ويندوز أو أوبونتو، واختيار أيقونة أرتب على الأجهزة اللوحية.
- **ويندوز:** اختر أيقونة التطبيق (خمسة تصاميم وتسعة ألوان) مع أيقونة أكبر في شريط المهام. تفتح القائمة بجانب الصفحة وتبقى مفتوحة. فعّل هاتفك عبر USB خطوة بخطوة، مع بطاقة لهاتفك أعلى صفحة الهاتف. يظهر تنبيه منطقة الإشعارات مرة واحدة فقط، ولم تعد هناك حالة الاستعداد. يتضمن أندرويد 0.5.8.
- لينكس يبقى 0.5.7.

</div>

## Linux 0.5.7

- EasyEffects: when EasyEffects plays into Sukut (its "Use default output" on), Sukut plays to your real speakers and remembers them, with no loop warning.
- Apps that EasyEffects processes are no longer reported as skipping Sukut when EasyEffects plays into Sukut.
- Turning Sukut off no longer leaves the sound silent with EasyEffects.

## 0.5.6

**Android**

- After you activate, Sukut starts filtering by itself.
- The Debugging tile turns debugging on and reconnects in the background, without opening Sukut.
- Phone activation returns to Sukut as soon as the code is accepted, retries by itself, and reconnects when Wi-Fi comes back. It needs Wi-Fi; a hotspot without internet works.
- The activation screen closes by itself when done; the full guide skips steps you've already done.
- Clearer error messages.

**All platforms**

- Nine app icon background colours, including teal, orange and raspberry.
- No more "Standby" status: Sukut simply stays on and rests by itself when nothing plays.

**Linux and Windows**

- A refreshed Phone page, led by a card for your phone (Windows: in the next Windows build).

## 0.5.2 (Android, Linux; Windows to follow)

- Choose your app icon: five designs and six background colours, in Settings. The default is the white and blue Seen on black.
- Android: a shorter activation guide, and the in-app icon no longer shows a dark frame.
- Linux: left-click the tray icon for the quick panel; right-click for Open and Exit. A refreshed Phone page.
- The "Sukut is in the tray" notice appears only the first time.

## 0.5.1 (Android, Linux; Windows to follow)

**All platforms**

- New app icon. The tray, the Android Quick Settings tile and the notification keep the س icon.

**Android**

- Activation one step at a time: choose Computer (recommended) or this phone.
- Clearer recovery when the audio worker stops, with an optional Debugging tile (new icon).
- Fixed filtering on Android 12 and 13 ([#1](https://github.com/altarafiyah/Sukut/issues/1)).
- The Quick Settings tile now starts Sukut while the app is closed.
- Corrected Arabic wording and grammar.

**Linux**

- Stable 0.5.1 as `.deb` (Ubuntu 26.04+, PikaOS 4), Flatpak and snap.
- Right-click the tray icon for a quick panel with power, modes, mix, buffering, output and source app (on supported Wayland desktops; GNOME uses its own tray menu).
- Bundles Android 0.5.1 for the Phone page.

## Linux 0.4.0 beta 11 (Snap)

- Icons now show on KDE Plasma and other desktops whose icon theme isn't in the snap.

## Linux 0.4.0 beta 10 (Snap)

- Sukut is on the Snap Store, in the beta channel. It brings its own libraries, so it doesn't depend on your system's PipeWire version.
- If the snap isn't allowed to use audio yet, setup and the main window show one command to copy.

## Linux 0.4.0 beta 8 (Flatpak)

- First Flatpak build of Sukut for Linux, as one file that installs across distributions.
- The first time the Phone page needs it, Sukut downloads Google's adb and checks it.
- Start with system isn't available in the Flatpak yet.
- On computers where USB can't be opened from the sandbox, the Phone page points to Wireless debugging (Android 11+).

## Linux 0.4.0 beta 7 (Windows 0.4.2 and Android 0.4.2 unchanged)

**Linux (beta)**

- New headphones (Bluetooth or USB) are used as soon as they connect, with a notification.
  When they disconnect, Sukut goes back to the previous output.
- If your chosen output is missing when you press Start, Sukut plays on the default output
  and moves to it when it appears.
- If you change the output in your sound settings while Sukut is off, the next Start uses it.
- Picking the current output in your sound settings no longer lets apps skip Sukut.
- EasyEffects: Sukut stops an audio loop at once and shows how to fix it. Apps pinned to
  Easy Effects Sink now go through Sukut and get their pin back when it stops.
  **Using EasyEffects** under Audio output shows the setup steps.
- Built on PikaOS 4; a Debian 13 package will follow.

## Windows 0.4.2 · Android 0.4.2 · Linux 0.4.0 beta 6

**Windows**

- Bluetooth headphones and speakers work, including switching to them while filtering is on.
- When a new audio device connects, Sukut switches to it automatically and shows a notification.
- Turning Sukut off returns Windows sound to your current device, even if an earlier one was disconnected.
- Tray: a background-level slider for Optimized mode, and a buffering icon that matches your choice.
- New desktop layout: native navigation, boxed modes, app cards with icons, one help button,
  a shorter setup and a five-step phone setup.

**Android**

- Sukut runs its own audio worker; Shizuku is no longer needed. Activate it on the phone
  with Wireless debugging (Android 11+), or over USB from Sukut on Windows or Linux.
- Pair directly from setup, recover a stale worker after reinstalling, and fixed duplicated
  audio in screen recordings on Android 16.
- New package name `com.altarafiyah.sukut`; tablet layouts, notification and Quick Settings controls.

**Linux (beta)**

- First Linux release: GNOME-style window, tray, PipeWire routing, source-app filtering,
  Standby and the Phone page, in English and Arabic.
- The Phone page grants USB access with one system prompt, so no terminal is needed.
- Built on PikaOS 4; a Debian 13 package will follow.
