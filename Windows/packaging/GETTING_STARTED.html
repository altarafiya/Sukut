Sukut · سُكوت

Enjoy the internet. Without the music.

استمتع بالإنترنت، بلا موسيقى.

Extract the complete folder and open Sukut.exe. Follow the illustrated guide inside the app. Exit any older tray instance first.

The guide covers VB-CABLE, your speakers/headphones, volume keys and buffering. The Phone page installs or updates Sukut and activates its own Android audio worker over USB. No Shizuku app is required. Activation leaves audio Off; press Start on the phone.
