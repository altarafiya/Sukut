Sukut — Windows 0.4.2
1 November 2026

- Fixed Bluetooth playback selection and switching while filtering is running.
- Automatically selects a newly connected audio output and shows a notification.
- Stopping Sukut returns Windows playback to the selected device, also when an older device was disconnected.
- The tray shows an Optimized-mode background slider; the change applies when you release it.
- The tray's audio-buffering icon and colour match the selected preset.
- The tray and main window stay in sync; tray resources are released when it closes.
- Includes signed Android 0.4/code54/com.altarafiyah.sukut and USB activation for its audio worker.

Extract the complete folder and open Sukut.exe. Exit an older Sukut tray instance first.
The model, gains and buffer durations are unchanged.
See VERIFICATION.json for completed checks and their limits. No new battery, RAM or latency percentage is claimed.
